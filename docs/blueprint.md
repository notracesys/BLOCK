# **App Name**: Arraiá Gigante

## Core Features:

- Instant Digital Assembly Kit: Provides the 3m x 2.5m digital file partitioned into standard A4/A3 formats for easy home or print-shop output.
- AI Party Planning Assistant: A conversational tool that uses AI to provide decoration advice, music playlists, and recipe suggestions based on your guest list size.
- Interactive Montage Preview: An interactive UI element that shows a scale mockup of the panel next to human figures for real-size visualization.
- 5-Step Animated Workflow: A high-impact visual section detailing the path from purchase to the finalized decorated wall.
- Smart Redirect WhatsApp Checkout: Strategic floating CTA button that leads users directly to conversion agents via WhatsApp.
- Limited-Time Urgency Tracker: Dynamic visual indicators and banners showing real-time scarcity for the current promotion price.

## Style Guidelines:

- The primary brand color is Red Junino (#D72638). This is paired with an accent of Amarelo Milho (#F7B500) for high-impact calls to action and buttons.
- Secondary support colors include Azul Bandeirinha (#2962FF) and Laranja Fogueira (#FF7A00) used sparingly for decorative accents like digital 'flags'.
- Backgrounds will use a very light beige (#F9F5F0) to evoke the texture of premium stationery or raw wood without causing visual fatigue.
- Headline and body font: 'Poppins' used in bold weights for headings and medium/light for content to achieve a precise, contemporary, and professional look.
- Mobile-first vertical scroll with high-density conversion cards, maintaining ample white space and clear separation of concepts to prevent 'visual noise'.
- Hand-sketched yet refined icons depicting sunflowers, corn, and traditional flags to create a warm, artisanal feel within a modern framework.
- Smooth, physics-based scroll triggers and entrance fades for the benefit cards, ensuring the premium product perception is maintained.
